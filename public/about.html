About Signify

Bridging conversations. Building an inclusive world.

Signify

A smarter way to communicate with sign language.

Signify is an AI-powered communication platform that translates between speech, text and sign language, enabling inclusive and accessible conversations for everyone.

“ Different languages.

Our Mission

To break communication barriers and create an inclusive society where everyone can connect, learn and communicate easily through sign language and technology.

Our Vision

A world where people with hearing and speech disabilities can communicate, access information, and participate fully in every aspect of life.
